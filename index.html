Segregation rate is the weight-weighted share of audited waste in the correct bin. Diversion is correctly binned food, paper, plastic and metal as a share of all waste. Windows compare equal-length, non-overlapping periods (for example days 1 to 14 against the 14 days before them), so 7, 14 and 28 day views all have a comparison. Forecast uses that weekday’s average over the last 28 days, scaled by the last-7-day trend (limited to ±15%). The backtest hides the last 7 days, predicts them from earlier data only, and reports weighted error next to a “repeat last week” baseline. Ledger: collected = recycled + composted + rejected + disposed + unreconciled, using facility receipts from batch records; demo batches are seeded for older days. Records are stored in this browser only, with a hash chain for tamper checks. A real deployment needs server-side logins and storage.
